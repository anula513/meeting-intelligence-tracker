# User Archetype

**Name:** Maya Patel
**Role:** Project manager at a 40-person software company
**Context:** 10-12 meetings a week across three projects. She takes rough notes, and often nobody else does.

## Pain points
- Decisions are made quickly and buried in conversation
- Weeks later she can't remember what was decided, in which meeting, or whether it was final
- Stakeholders say "I thought we agreed on X" and she has no fast way to check
- Follow-ups slip because the owner was never clearly recorded

## What she does today
Scrolls through old notes, searches Slack, or asks colleagues.

## What success looks like
She pastes in a transcript and gets a clean list of decisions with status and source. Later she asks "What did we decide about the vendor?" and gets a cited answer in seconds.

## Design implications
- **Status field:** Maya needs to know what is final versus still open
- **Condition field:** "yes, if legal approves" is different from "yes"
- **Owner only when stated:** she will act on owners, so invented names are harmful
- **Source quotes and citations:** she must be able to verify before relying on an answer
